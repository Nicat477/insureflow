InsureFlow is an independent educational portfolio demonstration of insurance workflows. It is not an insurer and does not process real insurance contracts or payments.

Account and Google sign-in data

If you sign in with Google, the application requests basic OpenID Connect identity information (openid, email, profile). Supabase Auth receives the account identifier, email address and available basic profile information such as display name and profile image. This information is used to authenticate your account and associate it with a separate demo workspace. InsureFlow does not request Gmail messages, contacts, Drive files or other Google account content. Google passwords are not provided to InsureFlow.

Storage and use

Supabase Auth manages identity and authentication sessions. The application uses the verified account identifier to associate workspace records with the account in Supabase PostgreSQL. Browser storage may retain session information and preferences such as language and theme. The hosting and authentication services may process technical request and security logs. Demo records are intended only for fictional examples; do not enter real customer, financial, health or insurance information.

Service providers and sharing

Google provides optional identity authentication, Supabase provides authentication and database services, and Render hosts the application. If email/password authentication is used, Mailjet may process email addresses and authentication messages for delivery. The demo's Emlax, Bitrix24 and 1C integrations are simulations, not actual external insurance integrations. Google identity information is used for account access, not advertising or sale.

Retention, access and deletion

Account and workspace information may remain stored while an account exists. You may request access, correction or deletion by contacting the project maintainer through the support email shown on the Google consent screen or through the repository contact route below. Do not post account details publicly. The workspace table is designed to delete its associated record when the Supabase Auth account is deleted; provider logs and backups may have separate retention periods. You can also revoke Google access from your Google account settings. Revoking access does not by itself delete your InsureFlow account.

Security and contact

The demo uses authenticated account access and separate workspaces. It is not intended for sensitive or production data. For privacy requests, contact the maintainer using the support email in the Google consent screen, or request a private contact channel via the project repository without including personal details.
